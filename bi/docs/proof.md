# Proof for an answer

An answer waiting for an admin, such as "Explained: the payment declined", can
be followed up before it is decided. The person who gave the answer is asked
for proof: a MoMo screenshot or statement, a terminal slip, a bank statement,
a receipt or invoice, or something else.

## Who can ask

Admins, always. An admin can let a supervisor ask too: Settings › Rules ›
access, **Ask for proof** (*See* shows the proof sent; *See and act* lets them
ask). It is in neither preset.

## Asking

On the answer's card (Inbox › Waiting for admin, or the exception on its
shift) press **📎 Ask for proof**. Tap what to send, add a line saying exactly
what ("the MoMo message showing the 9:51pm payment was declined"), and
**Send the request**. Approve and Reject stay: you can decide without waiting.

The request goes to whoever gave the answer:

- **A supervisor's answer in Insight:** their Front desk › Shifts › To sort out › **Proof asked**,
  and an email. The card of the answer itself also has a **Send it here** box
  for them, wherever they open it, so they can send from there too.
- **A staff member's answer from HIVE (My till):** their **To sort out** in
  HIVE, and a HIVE notice (push and email, "Proof was asked for something you
  answered on your till list").

While it waits, the card says what was asked, of whom and when, with
**Remind** and **Cancel the request**.

## Sending it

The person asked takes a picture or chooses pictures or PDFs (up to six). A
picture is made smaller on the phone. A PDF can be up to about 1.4 MB; a
bigger one is sent as a photo of the page. They add a note and send. If they
cannot get it, **Say why instead** sends their reason.

## Back on the card

The proof shows on the answer's card with small previews that open full size,
and stays there for good. Then Approve, Reject, or **Ask for more**.

## Reminders

Each morning (with the 06:45 summary run) a request still waiting since the
day before is reminded, three times at most. Once the answer is decided
(approved, rejected or settled) the request closes by itself: nobody is
reminded for something already decided.
