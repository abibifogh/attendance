# Team

**Reports › Team** shows admins what is on each person's table, in every
app, and what is holding them back. Only admins see it.

Everybody active in HIVE is on it. A person is matched across the apps by
the email on their HIVE login; float receipts by their exact name. Somebody
with no email in HIVE shows only what HIVE knows.

## Where it reads from

| App | What it reads |
|---|---|
| HIVE | shifts worked, late and missed; the rota ahead; leave, advances, medical claims and swaps they asked for; sick notes owed; attendance queries |
| Maintenance and Team Tasks | jobs, planned and service jobs, rounds, deep cleans, store counts, store-change asks, tasks, week plans (a signed call, `activity`) |
| The POS | shifts, spends, orders, counts, shop counts, charges (a signed call, `/team/activity`) |
| The breakfast app | store-room requests they made |
| Insight | to-dos given to them, To sort out items given to them, proof asked of them, float receipts |

If an app does not answer, the page says "Not read just now" and shows the
rest. Answers are kept for two minutes.

## Everybody

**Day**, **Week** (Monday to Sunday) or **Month**, with ‹ › to move. Tiles
across the top: overdue, waiting on an admin, due, done (and how much on
time), on leave. Then a row each: how they are today, doing, due, overdue,
done, asked for, their rating, and what is holding them back. The most
behind and held back come first. A department filter is at the top right.

## One person

Press a row. Four columns: **Overdue**, **Doing now**, **Coming up** (shifts
too), **Done**. Under them, **Holding them back** (who or what they are
waiting on, and for how many days) and **What they asked for** (waiting,
approved or refused). A month adds a calendar: each day done on time, late,
missed, or off.

**On time** means done by its due date. Days the thing was held by somebody
else (parts, an approval, the store room) are added to the due date first,
so nobody is late because of somebody else.

## Evaluation

The **Evaluation** tab on a person: the last three months, against the three
months before. A score out of 5 and a word for it (Strong, Good, Solid,
Needs support, Struggling; improving, steady or slipping), and where they
stand among people in the same role.

The eight measures, each 1 to 5:

| Measure | From |
|---|---|
| Turns up | shifts worked against shifts on the rota |
| On time | clock-ins on time |
| Gets it done | done by the due date |
| Gets through work | done per shift, against others in the same role |
| Answers | how fast they answer to-dos, proof asked, attendance queries |
| Done right first time | jobs and plans not reopened or sent back |
| Follows the routine | rounds, week plans, counts and shifts closed on their day |
| Money and stock | float receipts, counts and charges not refused, short or charged |

A measure with fewer than three things to judge shows a dash: too little to
judge, not a low mark.

**Patterns** Insight looks for: late on one weekday more than others; one
kind of work taking much longer than for others (1.8 times or more);
routine work late at the end of the month; routine never missed; more or
less done month on month; overdue work mostly waiting on somebody else.
Each pattern gives something **to talk about**.

The **last six months**: how many things done each month, on time and late.

**Your notes**: what was talked about and agreed. Only admins see them.
**Print for an appraisal** prints the page.

## How the rating is worked out

At the bottom of Everybody. All eight measures count the same to begin
with. An admin can:

- give each measure a weight: not counted, ×0.5, ×1, ×1.5, ×2 or ×3;
- **add a measure** of their own (for example, guest complaints) and score
  each person on it by hand, 1 to 5, on their evaluation;
- remove a measure they added;
- **let people see their own evaluation**. Off to begin with. When on, each
  person finds **My evaluation** under their name, without the notes.
