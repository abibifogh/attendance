# Finding your way around

Insight has seven places. On a computer they run down the left, Settings at the
foot. On a phone six are the buttons along the bottom, and Settings is under your
name (top right).

| Place | Pages |
|---|---|
| **Today** | My list (your one list, by when each thing is due; see assistant.md) · Approvals (everything waiting for an admin, in every system) · Notifications (your inbox, and push to your phone; see notifications.md) |
| **To sort out** | Everything to act on from the shifts and Odoo: drawer, card & bank, POs, to-dos, Nice Place, Odoo |
| **Front desk** | Shifts (with Closing reports) · Cashiers (each cashier's drawer) · Safe · Nice Place (Counters & money, Pickups) |
| **Buying & stock** | Floats · Receipts · Spend · Stock from Odoo (admins; see stock-from-odoo.md) · Fixed assets (admins; see fixed-assets.md) · Suppliers |
| **Team** | Everybody (admins; see team.md) · Evaluations (everybody's rating, by role) · Staff costs |
| **Reports** | Money (Profit & loss, Health, Cash & collections) · Management report · Guests (Occupancy & spend, Prepaid stays) |
| **Settings** | People & access (Who sees what, People; see access.md) · Who gets what (People to ask, HIVE alerts, Management report, Prepaid summary) · Rules (Front desk, Nice Place, Receipts, Stock, Team rating) · Connections & files (Connections, Speed, Shift files) · Shared reports |

**Who gets what › People to ask** is every "this goes to a person" in one table:
the kitchen's store requests, POs with a stand-in vendor or item, and "Which
store?" for stock from Odoo, each with who it goes to and how they are told; and
what goes to everybody in a role (Nice Place shifts, the 07:00 list).

A page with more than one part has tabs across its top. Somebody given only
some of these sees only those; an uploader gets Upload files, and somebody
given nothing in Insight gets the other systems.

## Along the top

- **Search** (or Ctrl K, ⌘K on a Mac): any page by name, a supplier, or
  something to do (New PO, Give money, Count the safe, View as somebody else,
  Light or dark). Arrows move, Enter opens, Escape closes.
- **The days**: one window for every report page, kept in the address.
- **New PO** (admins).
- **The bell**: your unread notifications; opens Today › Notifications.
- **The apps button**: the other systems you were given, opened signed in.
  This used to be the Hub page.
- Your name (bottom left, or top right on a phone): View as somebody else,
  Light or dark, My evaluation (when an admin lets people see their own),
  Sign out.
