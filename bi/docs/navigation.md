# Finding your way around

Insight has five places. On a computer they run down the left; on a phone
they are the five buttons along the bottom.

| Place | Pages |
|---|---|
| **Assistant** | Approvals (everything waiting for an admin, in every system) · Brief |
| **Front desk** | Shifts (with To sort out and Closing reports) · People · Safe · Nice Place |
| **Buying** | Suppliers · Receipts · Floats · Spend |
| **Reports** | Money (Profit & loss, Health, Cash & collections) · Management report · Guests (Occupancy & spend, Prepaid stays) · Staff costs |
| **Settings** | People & access (People, Reports they see) · Connections & files (Connections, Shift files) · Rules · Shared reports |

A page with more than one part has tabs across its top. Somebody given only
some of these sees only those; an uploader gets Upload files, and somebody
given nothing in Insight gets the other systems.

## Along the top

- **Search** (or Ctrl K, ⌘K on a Mac): any page by name, a supplier, or
  something to do (New PO, Give money, Count the safe, View as somebody else,
  Light or dark). Arrows move, Enter opens, Escape closes.
- **The days**: one window for every report page, kept in the address.
- **New PO** (admins).
- **The apps button**: the other systems you were given, opened signed in.
  This used to be the Hub page.
- Your name (bottom left, or top right on a phone): View as somebody else,
  Light or dark, Sign out.
